A hand-drawn procedural treasure map of the fictional **Muddlewick Isle**, surveyed by Captain T. Wobble in 1743 (he got lost twice).

## Map Features & Charm
- **Port Nonsense**: The bustling starting harbour with the tiny vessel *"HMS Eventually"* at anchor.
- **The Giggling Cliffs**: Towering rocky bluffs on the western shore where laughing gulls echo rude noises.
- **Mudlark Marsh**: Lowland squelchy wetlands featuring cattails, puddles, and ye lost boot stuck fast in the mire.
- **Mount Oops**: An active volcanic ridge near the north, flying a little flag from its grumbling summit.
- **River Chuckle**: A winding river flowing from the slopes of Mount Oops eastward into the sea.
- **Captain Crumpet's Cove**: A sheltered cove on the east coast where tea is served daily at 4 bells.
- **The Very Suspicious Palm**: A solitary palm tree with shifty eyes that was definitely not there yesterday.
- **The Pickled Parrot's Treasure**: The final destination marked with a bold red **X**, containing 10,000 doubloons and a salty cracker.
- **Cartographic Route**: A dotted red trail leading intrepid navigators safely from Port Nonsense across the island to the treasure.
- **Sea Denizens & Curiosities**:
  - *Sir Reginald, a Mildly Annoyed Squid* savoring a quiet steep of tea with his monocle.
  - *The Polite Serpent (says sorry)* apologizing for his wake in the northwest sea.
  - *Barnaby the Dapper Crab* wearing a tiny top hat on the sandy southern beach.
  - A friendly mermaid waving from the southwest waters.
  - An ornate 8-point compass rose with North indicated by a fleur-de-lis.

## Program Entrypoint
`main.ts` writes `entry.png` deterministically to the output directory (`process.env.MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` or `/output`).
